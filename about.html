About Reece

An engineer by trade, a pianist by heart, and somehow always travelling with a piano.

Established 1991 in an English seaside town, I've been fortunate enough to travel some of the world pursuing an enjoyment for engineering whilst managing to haul a piano with me.

I began learning the keyboard, and then piano. Having always had a curiosity for stringed instruments I thought I'd try to keep it simple and started with the ukulele, eventually falling in love with that instrument too.

Now based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, I am preparing new recordings and creative projects.
