About Reece

Engineer by trade. Pianist by heart. Always travelling with a piano.

Born in 1991 in an English seaside town. Engineering carried me around the world, and somehow a piano always came too.

Keyboard first, then piano. Curious about strings, I kept it simple with the ukulele, and fell for that too.

Now in Abu Dhabi, working on new recordings and whatever comes next.
